# PopkaStudio — working notes for agents

ESP32 watch firmware (`firmware/PopkaWatch`, Arduino C++), its UI library (`lib/Halo`), and a browser editor
(`studio/`: `studio.py` server + `web/` page that runs the firmware itself as WebAssembly). The round 1.75″ AMOLED
watch (`amoled175`, 466 × 466, 1 dp ≈ 2 px) is the main target; four rectangular boards share the code.

## Where things are
- `firmware/PopkaWatch/*.h` — one header per screen/app, included in order from `PopkaWatch.ino`. The face is made of
  layers (`face.h`: `FL_*` kinds; `layers.h` is generated by `studio.py`). Paged apps sit on the kit `upager.h`
  (pages, rim ring, page dots, curved time; list mode = Wear OS scrolling lists); settings-style lists on `ui.h`.
- `lib/Halo/src` — drawing (bands, items that track their own damage), touch, lists (`halo_list.cpp`), power.
- `studio/studio.py` — the editor's server and the build: `write_sketch(project, folder)` writes a buildable sketch
  (layers.h, config.h, wifi_net.h, fonts) from a project JSON. New layer kind = `KINDS` + defaults here,
  `KIND_INDEX`/`KINDS` in `studio/web/js/state.js`, the `FL_*` enum in `face.h` (same number), its code.
- `docs/references/` — Wear OS design guides and Pixel Watch faces: the design target. Check screens against them.

## The loop (no studio window needed)
```
studio/engine/build.sh amoled175 build-x round          # firmware → wasm (scratch folder; build/ is the shipped one)
ENGINE=studio/web/build-x node tools/watch/render.mjs /tmp/r "tap 233,355; wait 60; shot wx; cmd upcheck"
python3 tools/watch/sheet.py /tmp/sheet.png /tmp/r/*.png   # side by side, cut to the circle (--guides)
.venv/bin/python tools/watch/flash.py                     # build the user's project, flash, watchdog-reset
.venv/bin/python tools/watch/drive.py c:wake w:1 "c:tap 233 360" w:3 s:/tmp/wx.png   # the real panel
```
- `upcheck` (serial or `cmd upcheck`) audits the paged app in front: overlapping ink, text near the rim.
- Navigate apps by name: Apps list in front (`"c:drag 233 300 233 40"`), then `"c:open Radio"` — never blind
  coordinate taps on a list (it keeps its scroll; a blind tap once landed on Power off).
- `wxt` (serial, once Weather has opened) prints the forecast's start against the watch's clock — the hours shown hang on both.
- Before committing firmware that the editor shows, rebuild `studio/web/build` (`studio/engine/build.sh`) and commit
  `PopkaWatch.js/.wasm` with `git add -f` (they are tracked under an ignore rule).
- Compile all boards: `write_sketch` per board then arduino-cli — see `tools/watch/flash.py --build-only` for one;
  the raw `firmware/PopkaWatch` folder does not build on the rectangular boards.

## Gotchas
- arduino-cli needs `ARDUINO_DIRECTORIES_USER=.tools/arduino-s3` (the ESP32 core lives there); flash.py sets it.
- After an upload the S3 can stay in ROM download mode (dark screen, silent serial): `flash.py --reset` (watchdog).
  Never toggle DTR/RTS by hand.
- Locked watch (dim, shrunk face; a PWR click locks): `drive.py "c:boot long"` unlocks; taps do nothing while locked.
- Other agents share the board and `main`: `lsof /dev/cu.usbmodem*` before flashing (flash.py refuses a busy port).
- Pixels outside the circle in wasm renders are stale band leftovers, not bugs.

## Design rules (round screen; sources in docs/references and the Wear OS Compose M3 code)
- Margins: 5.2 % sides, 10 % top/bottom; list top ≥ 62 px; time text at 12 o'clock, titles under it (y ≈ 98).
- Lists: 410 px full pills, 8 px apart, shrink to 0.6 and fade at the rim (`upager.h` list mode / `halo_list`).
- Page dots on the rim at 6 o'clock; rim arcs leave them a gap (`up_arc` does). The rim ring only while it carries
  progress, on the first page. Scroll indicator at 3 o'clock.
- Optical centre ≈ 16 px above the middle; centre lopsided marks halfway between box centre and ink centroid
  (hang a ° outside the digits). One type cut per role (time: the 103 px display cut; heroes 154 px).
- House tokens: cards `0x2E3034`, text `0xFFF8EE`, secondary `0x9E9E9E`, each app its accent + a tonal container.
- Settings and choices are rows, never a card holding blocks: `up_row()` in `upager.h` (a full pill per control —
  plain button, switch or radio, filled with the accent when picked; `UP_ROW_X0/X1/H` on fixed pages). A number to
  step is a stepper/slider pill: − and + discs at the ends, the value between. Quick controls are round icon
  toggles (Pixel Watch's quick settings), not tiles. Ref: `docs/references/wear-os/surfaces/apps/layouts/scrolling/`
  (settings, toggle-buttons) and `m2-5/components/toggle-chips/`.
- One card language: a card's name in `gl_row_lab` (28 px, 620), the line under it in `gl_row_sub` (22 px, 450,
  `0x9E9E9E`), 32 px apart, the pair centred — settings rows, the Apps list, Weather's cards. Checked rows sit on the
  accent's tonal container (Wear OS's primary container), never a bright accent fill.
- The one exception: live readings are Wear OS progress cards — the label small in the accent, the reading big, a ring
  or chart at the right (Weather's Humidity/Wind/UV, About's Processor/Memory…). Keep them; don't turn them into rows.
- Volume is one screen, `up_volume()` in `upager.h` (Wear OS's volume screen: + on top, − below, the output chip
  between, the level on the left rim); percentages are written `60%`, no space.
- Hold to unlock is the whole rim glowing, deeper as the hold fills (`power.h`; serial `unlockpeek 0..1 | off` shows it).
- Frame rate: the log's "N fps drawn" counts only frames with changes; measure with motion filling the whole second.
- Render every change, then check it on the device: `upcheck` for overlaps, `sheet.py --guides` for balance.

## Conventions
- Commit and push every UI iteration as soon as it builds, even before it is seen on the watch (say which in the
  body): the history is how renders are compared iteration to iteration.
- Comments explain *why*, in the codebase's voice (parenthetical asides, dates of user decisions).
- Commit messages: `feat(watch): …` / `fix(…)`, a body listing what each file got.
